WELCOME TO BEACON

A place where every child can shine.

Welcome to The New Beacon School System, Larkana Campus, located behind the Arts Council, Larkana. We are committed to providing quality education in a safe, caring, and inspiring environment where students can learn, grow, and achieve their full potential.

Our school provides education from Playgroup (PG) to Matriculation, with a strong focus on academic excellence, character building, discipline, confidence, and personal development.
